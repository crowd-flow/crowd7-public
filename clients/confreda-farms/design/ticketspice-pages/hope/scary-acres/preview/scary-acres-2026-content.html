Enter If You Dare The Attractions Reinvented. Reinvested. Relentless. New sets, new monsters, and experiences built to push the limits of your fear.
The Dark Harvest Hayride Climb aboard for Rhode Island's most terrifying haunted hayride, deep into cursed farmland where the harvest is not crops — but souls. Vengeful scarecrows, restless farmers, shrieking chainsaws and fields alive with things that should never grow.
The Burial Ground Corn Field Step off the path into cursed rows of corn where the dead don't rest quietly. Legend says this land was once a graveyard — and when the corn grew over it, the restless souls rose with it. Nowhere to run. Only forward.
The Curse of the Bayou Into the swamp where the fog never lifts and the whispers never stop. A dark ritual woke what sleeps in these wetlands. Beware the voodoo priestess who guards this haunted ground — her curse ensures no soul leaves unscathed.
The Blood Moon Chapel More than a haunted house — a cathedral of fear. Step inside and you're surrounded by unsettling chants, flickering candlelight, and the tortured souls of a congregation that should have stayed buried.
