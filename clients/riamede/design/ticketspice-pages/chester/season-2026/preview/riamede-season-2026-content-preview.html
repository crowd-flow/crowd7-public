Important Things to Know

🍎 Kids ages 2 and under are free and don't require a ticket!

Produce & flowers: All produce and flowers are priced separately from admission — your ticket covers entry to the open picking fields. Please bring a reusable bag to pick into; if you don't have one, we have free paper picking bags at the gate and even stronger cloth bags available for purchase.

Weekends vs. weekdays: The hay wagon, apple slingshot, Orchard Grill, Sweet Shed, farm animals, and live music run on weekends only — and the line-up changes every weekend. See the full weekend schedule for who’s playing and which animals are visiting. The apple slingshot carries a small additional fee, is cash-only, and opens in mid-September.

Pony rides & farm animals: Wagon rides and pony rides start Labor Day weekend. The ponies and farm animals visit Saturdays and Sundays only, in partnership with our visiting animal partners — they’re guests on the farm for the weekend, not Riamede’s own animals.

Hours: We're open 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM, Wednesday through Monday — closed Tuesdays (November: 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM). Last entry for picking is 4:00 PM. See full 2026 season hours.

Your Family Bundle basket: The commemorative basket is filled with farm-fresh apples and is redeemable for Fall Fest, September 1 through November 1. Due to a devastating spring frost event, we cannot guarantee that your basket of apples will be from our trees throughout the entire fall, but we have a good number of apples on the trees projected across the whole season and are hoping that they make it into your basket! If not, we'll be supplying from partner Eco-Certified farms nearby.

Pets: We do not allow pets or emotional support animals on the farm grounds.

Tickets: All ticket sales are final and non-refundable — but you can create an account and change the date of your tickets yourself, any time.
