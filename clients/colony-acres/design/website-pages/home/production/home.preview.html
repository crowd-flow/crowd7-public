Fall on the farm opens September 19 · North Liberty, Iowa

Our 100th season opens September 19, 2026 — and we can’t wait to welcome you back. A century ago our family planted roots here in North Liberty, and this fall we’re celebrating every one of those years: themed weekends all season long, our Centennial Bourbon Dinner, and all the traditions your family looks forward to. We were so blessed in 2025 by our friends and family, our loyal customers, and our community — and we’re ready to make our Centennial the most memorable year yet.
