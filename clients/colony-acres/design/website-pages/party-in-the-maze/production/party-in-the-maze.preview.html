Host a Private Event each Fall!

Additional Admissions can be purchased for your group. Looking for a unique private party location? Look no further than the Party Pavilions at Colony Acres.

Reserve your own private party pavilion located within our maze. Designed like an island, these spaces provide the perfect spot for your group to gather and celebrate in your space. Plus, you’re welcome to bring a few of your own party decorations to add a personal touch to the festivities. (Decor exclusions apply.)

Your $50+tax reservation includes:

  • Use of our private tented picnic spaces in our maze for 2 hours
  • 3 picnic tables and 1 garbage can
  • $50+ tax due with reservation.
  • Reservations can be made through the link below.

Admissions are additional and can be purchased at a discounted rate online or regular price at the gate. Admissions are non-date specific, but the party reservation is. Additional discounts for groups of 20+ when purchased as 1 transaction.

*Please Note: Party Pavilions are ONLY available during our Fall Harvest Season.  Contact us directly for private event inquiries outside of the fall season.
