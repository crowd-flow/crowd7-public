Fall Field Trips & Private Group Tours

In the Fall our private Group Tours are customized to your needs. Each group begins with a short educational, or farm history Q & A at your chosen time slot, then either free play (field trips) or a private tour until your group decides to leave! We are open to the public at the same time.

For an admission free option, and without the need for a reservation, your group is welcome to come out during regular open hours to enjoy farm fresh air, shopping, and goodies at our Barn and Market areas.

Fall Field Trip Reservation

Field trips will start at your reserved time with a short story time, then your group is free to enjoy the farm on your own! You can enjoy our ‘Spookley' story walk, many photo ops, a hayrack ride (if available), Fun Yard activities, and snack time!

  • This is a reservation. Choose 1 per total group.
  • Minimum of 10 people to a group, No Maximum
  • Cost is $10+tax & applicable fees/child, sibling, parent, chaperone. Educators are free with ID. A small gourd is included in the price.
  • School group is to be paid as 1 transaction, but siblings, parents, & chaperones pay separate upon arrival.
  • Field Trip Price is only available at the Gate, price difference refunds will NOT be given for WeekDay tickets purchased online

Fall Private Group Tour Reservation

Do you have a group from a retirement community, day hab program, private club, or similar and are looking for a fun fall day at the farm?

For a more personalized experience, and nominal admission fee, reserve a Private Group Tour that includes a private hayrack and history tour, plus all the time you want to enjoy the maze &/or Fun Yard!

  • This is a reservation. Choose 1 per total group.
  • Cost is $10+tax & applicable fees/person and can be paid upon arrival. A small gourd is included in the price.
  • Minimum of 10 people to a group, No Maximum
  • Price is only available at the Gate, price difference refunds will NOT be given for WeekDay tickets purchased online

 

Payment is due upon arrival at the farm, and field trips are expected to be paid as one (1) transaction.
