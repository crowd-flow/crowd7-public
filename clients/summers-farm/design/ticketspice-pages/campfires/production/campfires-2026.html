Good to know: Reservations are required for every campfire. Each visitor must purchase a farm ticket in addition to the campfire reservation — no exceptions. No alcohol is permitted at the campfires. Each campfire seats up to 50 and comes with sufficient wood, one picnic table, and two benches; groups are welcome to bring additional seating. Group pricing is available for 15+ tickets. Fireworks are every Saturday in October, plus Friday, October 16 and Friday, October 23.

Cancellations: A full refund is issued if the farm closes due to weather, or if you cancel at least 10 days prior to your reserved date. Cancellations within 10 days forfeit the reservation fee.
